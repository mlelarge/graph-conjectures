We study Conjecture~9.2 of Akbari--Kumar--Mohar--Pragada--Zhang
\cite{AKMPZ2025}, which asserts that for a connected graph $\graph$ of
order $n$, the negative square energy satisfies $\sminusG = n-1$ if and
only if $\graph$ is a tree or a complete graph. We focus on the class of
$2$-trees (maximal chordal graphs of treewidth $2$) and reduce the
conjecture for this class to an \emph{existential ear-selection lemma}
$\Lprime$. We prove $\Lprime$ unconditionally on books $\Bk$. On $2$-paths $\Ln$ we
prove the Szeg\H{o} Ces\`aro identity
$\sminus(\Ln)/n \to (32\pi - 27\sqrt{3})/(12\pi) \approx 1.4262$ for the
\emph{average} square energy per vertex; the corresponding
ear-gain statement $\dminus(\Ln) \ge 21/16$ is verified
finitely-rigorously for $n \le 1000$ by a Demmel--Kahan a-posteriori
certificate (conditional on a conservative dsyevd backward-error
envelope), and the asymptotic $\dminus(\Ln) \to c_{1}^{-}$ at the
first-difference level remains an open subobligation. On the structured $\BTkt{k}{2}$ family
we prove the bad-tail-ear asymptotic
$\lim_{k} \dminus(v_{\mathrm{tail}}) \approx 1.0353 < 17/16$, which
refutes the \emph{universal} ear-deletion form of $\Lprime$ and forces
the move to the existential / max-degsum selector formulation; the
existential $\Lprime$ on $\BTkt{k}{2}$ is verified empirically via the
max-degsum selector but is not analytically closed in this paper. We isolate the headline open
content as two precise conditions~(a),~(b) on a moment-form
joint-invariant candidate $\Iv = \Wm + (\Monem)^{2}/\Mtwom$, prove the
structural condition~(a) on two subfamilies (books and $2$-paths in
the asymptotic limit) together with a common-spine-edge
characterisation of the book family. On
condition~(b) we prove a Rayleigh-type bound
$\lambda_{\min}(\Adj(\graph))^{2} \ge 1$ on Case-B max-degsum ears
under an explicit moment sufficient condition; the headline
$\dminus(\maxdeg) \ge 17/16$ remains open and we identify the
slot-shift sum as its unified obstruction. The paper includes a new Rayleigh-quotient lower
bound on $\lambda_{\min}(\Adj(\graph))^{2}$, a two-sided
Cauchy--Schwarz structural lower bound on $\Iv$, and a
Stieltjes-transform identification of the $2$-path negative-spectrum
measure giving
$\Iinf{L} = (2(310\pi^{2} - 837\sqrt{3}\,\pi + 2187))/(27\pi(20\pi -
27\sqrt{3}))$. An appendix catalogues ten specific failure modes
rejected during the search, including the discovery that the $2$-path
is \emph{not} the universal binding case for $\Iv$ at finite~$n$.
