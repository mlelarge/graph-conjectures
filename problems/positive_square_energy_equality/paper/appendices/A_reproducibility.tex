\subsection{The working corpus}\label{app:repro:corpus}

The numerical claims of the paper rest on the following corpus of
$2$-trees:
\begin{itemize}
\item Enumerated $2$-trees with $n \in \{4, \ldots, 10\}$: $724$ graphs
(enumerated up to isomorphism by the standard 2-tree construction
\texttt{scripts/two\_tree\_enum.py}).
\item Random $2$-trees with $n \in \{15, 20, 30, 50, 100\}$, $50$ seeds
each: $250$ graphs (constructed by attaching each new vertex to a

\item $\BTkt{k}{2}$ for $k \in \{2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100\}$: $6$
structured graphs.
\item Books $\Bk$ for $k \in \{2, \ldots, 30\}$: $29$ graphs.
\item $2$-paths $\Ln$ for $n \in \{4, \ldots, 30\}$: $27$ graphs.
\item Fans $\Fn$ for $n \in \{4, \ldots, 30\}$: $27$ graphs.
\end{itemize}
Total: $1063$ graphs, $2628$ max-degsum ear records, $8890$ all-ear
records. The b.minor census of \Cref{prop:bminor-census} uses the
$2235$-record subset that excludes the random-$2$-tree contribution
(restricting to enumerated, books, BT, $L_{n}$, $F_{n}$).

\subsection{The Demmel--Kahan certificate}\label{app:repro:DK}

The precision protocol used to certify \Cref{thm:2path-DK}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Compute eigenvalues
$\tilde\lambda_{i}(\Adj(\Ln))$ for each $n \in [4, 1000]$ in IEEE-754
double precision via \texttt{numpy.linalg.eigvalsh}, whose default
backend is LAPACK's \texttt{dsyevd} (Householder tridiagonalisation
followed by symmetric divide-and-conquer).

\item Apply the Demmel--Kahan-style a-posteriori bound
\cite[Thm.~5.5]{DemmelANLA}: the forward error on each eigenvalue
satisfies $|\tilde\lambda_{i} - \lambda_{i}| \le C_{n} \cdot \|\Adj(\Ln)\|_{2}
\cdot \varepsilon_{\text{mach}}$ with $C_{n} \le c\, n$ for an absolute
constant $c$ from the backward-error analysis. We take the
conservative working envelope $c \le 10^{4}$ (overshooting published
constants for backward-stable symmetric eigensolvers by at least one
order of magnitude; see \Cref{rem:fp-vs-interval} for the honest
status of this constant — it is consistent with the literature on
the Householder-plus-divide-and-conquer pipeline used by
\texttt{dsyevd}, but a fully formal derivation of an explicit
\texttt{dsyevd}-specific envelope constant is beyond the scope of this
paper). With $\|\Adj(\Ln)\|_{2} \le \|f\|_{\infty} = 4$ the
per-eigenvalue bound is $\le 8.88 \cdot 10^{-12}\, n \le 8.88 \cdot
10^{-9}$ at $n \le 1000$, and the propagated $\tilde s_{n}^{-}$ error
is $\le 7.1 \cdot 10^{-5}$ uniformly across $n \in [4, 1000]$ (cf.\
\Cref{thm:2path-DK}).

\item The numerical slack is $\tilde\delta_{n}^{-} - 17/16 \ge 0.257$
for every $n \in [4, 1000]$, with minimum attained at $n = 6$. After
subtracting the propagated $\dminus$ forward-error bound
$\le 1.42 \cdot 10^{-4}$, the certified slack is
$\ge 0.257 - 1.5 \cdot 10^{-4} > 1/4$.
\end{enumerate}
The certificate is reproduced end-to-end by
\texttt{tests/test\_mpmath\_certify.py}. The stored mpmath companion
certificate at
\texttt{data/two\_path\_mpmath\_certificate.json} consists of $17$
records (a curated subset $n \in \{4, 5, \ldots, 10, 12, 15, 20, 30,
50, 80, 100, 130, 160, 200\}$ chosen to include the empirical
worst-case $n = 6$ together with a logarithmically-spaced tail) computed
at $50$ decimal digits via \texttt{mpmath.eigsy}; for runtime budget
the test suite spot-checks $9$ of these cases ($n \in \{4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10,
20, 50, 100\}$) at $30$ decimal digits, which still gives $\sim 25$
digits of headroom on every computed quantity. The full $50$-digit
records are present in the JSON for offline inspection. All checks
pass.

\subsection{The Stieltjes-transform identification}\label{app:repro:stieltjes}

The closed-form value
$\Iinf{L} = 2(310\pi^{2} - 837\sqrt{3}\pi + 2187)/(27\pi(20\pi - 27\sqrt{3}))$
of \Cref{thm:moment-form-stieltjes} is derived as follows.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Boundary density.} On the negative branch
$\lambda \in (-9/4, 0)$ the half-line spectral density of $\Adj(L_{\infty})$
tested against $\earw = e_{1} + e_{2}$ is the signed angle gap
$\rho_{w}(\lambda) = \sin(\theta_{2} - \theta_{1})/\pi$ (Step~3 of the
proof of \Cref{thm:2path-stieltjes}).

\item \emph{Moments.} Substituting $\lambda = f(\theta_{1})$ with
$\theta_{2} = \arccos(-1/2 - \cos\theta_{1})$, change of variables and
trigonometric integration give
\[
\Wm_{\infty} = 1 - \tfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4\pi},
\quad
\Monem[1, \infty] = \tfrac{2}{3} - \tfrac{9\sqrt{3}}{4\pi},
\quad
\Mtwom[2, \infty] = 3 - \tfrac{81\sqrt{3}}{20\pi}.
\]
The script \texttt{scripts/half\_line\_stieltjes.py} performs the
sympy computation symbolically and verifies against numerical
quadrature at $20$-digit precision.

\item \emph{Closed-form assembly.}
$\Iinf{L} = \Wm_{\infty} + (\Monem[1, \infty])^{2}/\Mtwom[2, \infty]$
simplifies to the displayed rational function of $\pi$ and $\sqrt{3}$.
Numerically $\Iinf{L} \approx 1.01574$, exceeding $\threshold = 0.4122$
by $\approx 0.604$.
\end{enumerate}
Reproducibility: the symbolic
$\Iinf{L}$ closed form, the moment expressions, the $G_{w} = s^{2} + s
- p$ collapse, and the spectral-density formula
$\rho_{w} = \sin(\theta_{2} - \theta_{1})/\pi$ are regression-locked at
the \texttt{sympy} level by
\texttt{tests/test\_half\_line\_stieltjes.py} (in particular,
\texttt{test\_symbolic\_moments\_match\_candidate\_closed\_form} and
\texttt{test\_I\_inf\_closed\_form} verify the symbolic differences are
exactly zero, and \texttt{test\_unsigned\_moments\_match\_matrix\_moments}
crosschecks the full-spectrum unsigned moments against
$(M_{0}, M_{1}, M_{2}) = (2, 2, 7)$ by quadrature). The finite-$n$
convergence harness for $\dminus(\Ln) \to \dminus_{\infty}$ on
$n \in [4, 200]$ is in
\texttt{tests/test\_two\_path\_widom\_tightness.py}.
