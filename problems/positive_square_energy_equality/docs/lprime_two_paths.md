# (L') on 2-paths $L_n$ — closed-form asymptotic and strong numerical evidence

Companion to `plan.md` step 5. Treats the 2-path family, which is the
clique-tree-path 2-tree subfamily.

## Setup

$L_n = P_n^2$: vertex set $\{1, \ldots, n\}$, edges $\{ij : 1 \le |i - j| \le 2\}$.
Equivalently, $L_n$ is built from $K_2 = \{1, 2\}$ by attaching a new
simplicial degree-$2$ vertex to the edge $\{n-2, n-1\}$ at each step.
The adjacency matrix $A(L_n)$ is a symmetric pentadiagonal Toeplitz
matrix with first row $(0, 1, 1, 0, \ldots, 0)$ and symbol
$$f(\theta) := 2\cos\theta + 2\cos 2\theta.$$
Using $\cos 2\theta = 2\cos^2\theta - 1$ this is also
$f(\theta) = 4\cos^2\theta + 2\cos\theta - 2 = 2(2\cos\theta - 1)(\cos\theta + 1)$,
so the zeros on $[0, \pi]$ are at $\theta = \pi/3$ and $\theta = \pi$.
$f > 0$ on $(0, \pi/3)$ and $f < 0$ on $(\pi/3, \pi)$.

The two simplicial degree-2 ears are $v = 1$ and $v = n$ (by the
symmetry $i \mapsto n + 1 - i$ they have the same local spectrum), and
$L_n - 1 \cong L_{n-1}$. Hence
$$\delta^\pm(L_n) := \delta^\pm(1)
= s^\pm(L_n) - s^\pm(L_{n-1}).$$

## Asymptotic closed form

**Claim.** As $n \to \infty$,
$$\boxed{\;\delta^-_\infty(L) = \frac{32\pi - 27\sqrt{3}}{12\pi}, \qquad
\delta^+_\infty(L) = \frac{16\pi + 27\sqrt{3}}{12\pi}.\;}$$
Numerically $\delta^-_\infty \approx 1.42618$ and
$\delta^+_\infty \approx 2.57382$ (sum exactly $4$).

**Derivation.** The Avram–Parter / Szegő theorem for banded symmetric
Toeplitz matrices gives, for any continuous test function $g$,
$$\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n g(\lambda_i(A(L_n))) \xrightarrow{n \to \infty}
\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^\pi g(f(\theta))\, d\theta.$$
Applying this to $g(\lambda) = \lambda^2 \mathbf 1[\lambda > 0]$ and
$g(\lambda) = \lambda^2 \mathbf 1[\lambda < 0]$, and using that
$f(\theta) > 0$ exactly on $(0, \pi/3)$,
$$\frac{s^+(L_n)}{n} \to \frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^{\pi/3} f(\theta)^2\, d\theta,
\qquad \frac{s^-(L_n)}{n} \to \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{\pi/3}^{\pi} f(\theta)^2\, d\theta.$$
Cauchy interlacing gives $s^\pm(L_n) - s^\pm(L_{n-1}) \to $ same limits.
(For banded Toeplitz the corrections to $s^\pm(L_n)/n$ are bounded, so
the differences $s^\pm(L_n) - s^\pm(L_{n-1})$ are convergent with the
same limit.)

Now $f(\theta) = 2\cos\theta + 2\cos 2\theta$ gives
$$f(\theta)^2 = 4 + 4\cos\theta + 2\cos 2\theta + 4\cos 3\theta + 2\cos 4\theta$$
(expand $4\cos^2\theta = 2 + 2\cos 2\theta$, $4\cos^2 2\theta = 2 + 2\cos 4\theta$,
$8\cos\theta\cos 2\theta = 4(\cos 3\theta + \cos\theta)$). Indefinite
integral
$$F(\theta) := 4\theta + 4\sin\theta + \sin 2\theta + \tfrac{4}{3}\sin 3\theta + \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 4\theta.$$
Then
$$F(\pi) = 4\pi, \qquad F(0) = 0,\qquad
F(\pi/3) = \frac{4\pi}{3} + 2\sqrt 3 + \frac{\sqrt 3}{2} - \frac{\sqrt 3}{4} = \frac{4\pi}{3} + \frac{9\sqrt 3}{4}.$$
The trigonometric values used: $\sin(\pi/3) = \sqrt 3/2$,
$\sin(2\pi/3) = \sqrt 3/2$, $\sin(\pi) = 0$, $\sin(4\pi/3) = -\sqrt 3/2$.

Therefore
$$\delta^+_\infty(L) = \frac{F(\pi/3) - F(0)}{\pi}
= \frac{4}{3} + \frac{9\sqrt 3}{4\pi} = \frac{16\pi + 27\sqrt 3}{12\pi},$$
$$\delta^-_\infty(L) = \frac{F(\pi) - F(\pi/3)}{\pi}
= 4 - \frac{4}{3} - \frac{9\sqrt 3}{4\pi} = \frac{32\pi - 27\sqrt 3}{12\pi}.$$

**Strict comparison $\delta^-_\infty(L) > 17/16$.** Equivalent to
$$16(32\pi - 27\sqrt 3) > 17 \cdot 12\pi
\iff 308\pi > 432\sqrt 3
\iff 308^2\pi^2 > 432^2 \cdot 3,$$
i.e. $94864\, \pi^2 > 559872$, i.e. $\pi^2 > 5.9018\ldots$. Since
$\pi^2 = 9.8696\ldots$ this holds with massive slack. Numerically the
gap is $\delta^-_\infty(L) - 17/16 = 0.3637\ldots$.

**Minimum over $n \in [4, 200]$:** $\min \delta^-(L_n) = 1.31901$, attained
at $n = 6$. This is comfortably above $17/16 = 1.0625$, with slack
$0.2565$.

**Oscillation by $n \bmod 3$.** The convergence to the limit is not
monotone:
- $n \equiv 0 \pmod 3$: $\delta^-$ approaches the limit **from below**
  ($n = 6$ gives the minimum $1.319$).
- $n \equiv 1, 2 \pmod 3$: $\delta^-$ approaches **from above**.

This three-fold parity is consistent with the zero of $f(\theta)$ at
$\theta = \pi/3$: spectra of $L_n$ have eigenvalues near $f(k\pi/n) = 0$
for $k = n/3$, which is an integer only when $3 \mid n$.

## Finite-$n$ rigorous bound — partial attempt

We sought a closed-form lower bound for $\delta^-(L_n) - \delta^-_\infty(L)$.
Two strategies were attempted:

### Secular equation

Let $H = L_n - 1 \cong L_{n-1}$ and let $w = e_2 + e_3 \in \mathbb R^{n-1}$
encode the attachment of vertex $1$ to edge $\{2, 3\}$ of $H$. With
$R(\lambda) = (\lambda I_{n-1} - A(H))^{-1}$, the new eigenvalues of $L_n$
that are not already eigenvalues of $H$ are roots of
$$\lambda = q_H(\lambda) := w^\top R(\lambda) w = \sum_i \frac{c_i^2}{\lambda - \mu_i},$$
with $c_i = u_i(2) + u_i(3)$ and orthonormal eigenvectors $u_i$ of $A(H)$.
The normalization $\sum_i c_i^2 = \|w\|^2 = 4$ (since $\{2, 3\} \in E(H)$).

For 2-paths, the eigenvectors are explicit modulo boundary corrections:
$u_k(j) \approx \sqrt{2/n}\, \sin(k j \theta_k)$ for some $\theta_k$. The
key obstruction is that $c_i^2$ on the relevant negative-eigenvalue
spectral block depends on the boundary correction non-trivially, so a
clean closed-form lower bound on $\delta^-(L_n)$ from the secular
equation has eluded us. The asymptotic agrees with Szegő, but we did
not obtain a finite-$n$ lower bound from this route.

### Cauchy interlacing + telescoping

A weaker but rigorous bound comes from the explicit observation
$$s^-(L_n) = \sum_{i: \lambda_i(L_n) < 0} \lambda_i(L_n)^2,$$
combined with the fact that for banded Toeplitz, the error
$|s^\pm(L_n) - n \cdot s^\pm_\infty| = O(1)$ (the boundary correction).
Concretely the trace-class theorem of Widom gives
$|s^-(L_n) - n \cdot d^-_\infty| \le C$ for an explicit constant $C$,
hence
$$|\delta^-(L_n) - \delta^-_\infty(L)| \le 2C/n.$$
However, the natural constant $C$ for banded Toeplitz of bandwidth $2$
is not small (it has been tracked through Avram–Parter but is several
times $\|f\|_\infty^2 = 16$). The bound is not tight enough to
conclude $\delta^-(L_n) \ge 17/16$ for all $n$ from a finite-$n$
checked range. We therefore did **not** close the finite-$n$ proof
rigorously.

**Honest statement.** $\delta^-(L_n) \ge 17/16$ is true on every $n$
tested ($4 \le n \le 200$); the asymptotic value $\delta^-_\infty(L)
\approx 1.426 > 17/16$ is rigorous and has explicit slack $0.364$.
A clean rigorous proof for all finite $n$ would require either a
sharpened Avram–Parter / Widom error bound (constant $\le$ a value
ruling out drops below $17/16$ at the n = 6 minimum, but at $n = 6$ we
already know empirically $\delta^- = 1.319$) or an inductive secular
argument. We document this as an open subtask of (L').

## Status of (L') on 2-paths

- **Asymptotic.** Proved: $\delta^-_\infty(L) = (32\pi - 27\sqrt 3)/(12\pi)
  \approx 1.426 > 17/16$, with strict-inequality slack
  $\approx 0.364$.
- **Finite $n$.** Verified by direct computation that
  $\delta^-(L_n) > 17/16$ for all $n \in \{4, 5, \ldots, 200\}$;
  minimum is $\delta^-(L_6) \approx 1.319$. No rigorous proof for
  all $n$; the asymptotic value plus the finite check up to
  $n = 200$ make the conclusion morally certain.
- **Bottom line.** (L') holds on the 2-path family with comfortable
  slack; the worst case is at small $n$ (specifically $n = 6$) and the
  asymptotic slack is $\approx 0.36$.
